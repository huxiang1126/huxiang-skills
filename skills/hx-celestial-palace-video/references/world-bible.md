# Celestial Palace World Bible

Use this as the invariant layer. Combine it with one row from `nine-shot-matrix.md`; do not ask the image model to invent all nine compositions in one grid.

## Core mood

天上之宫，不落人间；人如一笔，宫阙无涯；近处有巨物，远处有天境；静处显庄严，动处生仙气；空而不寂，满而不塞。

The image should feel like a large-format architectural photograph from an expensive live-action fantasy film: physically credible stone, jade, timber, tiles, fabric, water, cloud volume, moon surface, and perspective. It is bright, quiet, restrained, and immense—not a game loading screen, dense concept-art city, stage set, or decorative poster.

## Gigantic scale system

- A main column is 5–10 immortal figures wide and 40–80 figures tall.
- A gate, jade screen, or structural opening is 20–50 figures tall.
- A principal eave spans hundreds of body widths and continues beyond the frame.
- Colonnades, stairs, ridges, corridors, halls, platforms, and bridges repeat toward a clear vanishing point.
- Steps remain human-walkable while the whole stair crosses clouds and multiple altitude levels.
- A person occupies only a tiny fraction of a plinth, column base, screen, or gate.

Every image must show at least four separate scale proofs:

1. a giant architectural fragment leaving the frame;
2. extreme person-to-structure ratio;
3. repeated units shrinking toward a vanishing point;
4. empty space extending 10–30 body lengths around the person;
5. palace bodies at several altitudes;
6. depth crossing the cloud sea;
7. near and far giant structures together;
8. a distant structure shown only in part yet still enormous.

## Five depth layers and breathable fullness

Reserve about 40–60% of the frame for breathable space, but never as blank flat sky. It must contain atmospheric depth, value changes, light, or distant forms.

1. Near: a cropped giant column, eave, pine branch, stone screen, fabric edge, or platform mass.
2. Figure plane: a broad terrace, stair, corridor, mirror-water floor, or edge.
3. Primary mass: the dominant palace structure.
4. Distant architecture: one to three lighter, softer palace, bridge, wall, roof, or suspended-platform silhouettes.
5. Terminal distance: giant moon, diffuse light, heavenly river, galaxy, cloud sea, or boundless high sky.

Keep near forms heavy and explicit; far forms pale, soft, and summarized. Allow sky to pass through gates, columns, screens, and eaves.

## Distant anchors

Use only one or two per image and rotate them across the set:

- immense palaces suspended at different heights;
- an exceptionally long bridge between two giant palace bodies;
- halls receding along a low cloud-sea edge;
- a monumental gate silhouette lit from behind;
- a transparent waterfall from a high terrace into clouds;
- a distant curved palace wall and roof sequence;
- a heavenly river like a transparent ribbon;
- one crane crossing a giant moon, or very few distant birds;
- suspended platforms separated by air and value;
- only the eave, gate, screen, or colonnade of an even larger off-frame structure.

## Moon, light, and cloud sea

- Use a giant moon in three or four of the nine images. It occupies roughly 25–50% of frame width and has real maria, craters, terminator, and volume.
- The moon may be complete, cropped, behind a gate, beyond a corridor, or cut by architecture. Keep it low or off-axis rather than pasted at dead center.
- The sun remains low, distant, or off-frame. It supplies broad high-altitude diffuse light and a very thin rim, not a loud flare.
- The cloud sea lies below the principal platform level. Its top is below the figure's feet and platform edge.
- Clouds occupy mainly the lower 20–35%, with rounded real cumulus volume; upper architecture remains in clear high-altitude air.
- Platform edges open directly to cloud sea and depth. No earth horizon.

## One controlled dynamic layer

Choose one dominant phenomenon per image:

- a slow cloudfall pouring over a terrace into lower clouds;
- thin nearly colorless heavenly water falling from a high palace;
- several narrow distant waterfalls revealing vertical scale;
- a transparent heavenly river curving between buildings;
- one or two ripples after a footstep on a mirror-water floor;
- a broad banner, translucent veil, or eave ornament bent by one consistent high-altitude breeze;
- one crane crossing a distant palace or resting on a giant pine;
- water following a curved channel and falling from the platform;
- thin cloud flow moving under suspended construction.

Gravity is consistent: water and cloudfalls descend below platforms. Motion feels slow, continuous, and majestic.

## Giant welcoming pine

Use selectively, never as a compulsory decoration. Its trunk is 1–3 figure heights in diameter and its crown spans 20–40 body widths. A trunk or branch enters from outside the frame, shows old heavy bark, and can frame distant architecture. A crane may rest on one horizontal branch. Needles move gently while the trunk remains stable.

## Architecture

Use Chinese spatial order without copying a specific dynasty. Reorganize long eaves, giant columns, moon openings, monumental stone screens, corridors, stairs, layered roofs, cloud terraces, and suspended gates into a celestial language.

- Giant, simple, quiet masses.
- Broad intact material surfaces that receive light.
- Sparse structural lines and repeated elements for order and distance.
- Decoration concentrated in one area; the rest remains calm.
- Relief motifs abstract cloud paths, water, star tracks, moon halos, wind traces, and astronomical arcs.
- Materials rotate among pale massive stone, warm white marble, pearl-gray stone, restrained cinnabar/ochre timber, and thin pale-gold ridge lines.
- No railings or guardrails on any ground, bridge, stair, or platform.

## Ground relationships

Rotate among:

- a mirror floor with a very shallow transparent water film and one or two ripples;
- a vast edge-less reflecting platform;
- broad pale-stone stairs with large shallow relief;
- a clean suspended platform opening directly to cloud sea;
- a calm shallow channel along a curved corridor, ending in a waterfall;
- a broad empty jade plane that makes the person small.

No railing in any form.

## Figures and timeless celestial clothing

Show complete back views, 30–60-degree oblique rear views, rear-side walking, or one over-shoulder rear view. The viewer and figure look toward the same enormous destination.

Actions are quiet and autonomous: walking slowly, standing at an edge, ascending steps, entering a curved corridor, looking into distance, hands resting naturally, or lightly holding one sleeve. Bodies stay stable while hair, hem, and one or two long panels share one wind direction.

Clothing rule: 衣有骨、纱有气、饰有止、色有主。

- Inner layer: matte, dense, weighted, elongated.
- Middle layer: soft, slightly detached from the body, gently translucent.
- Outer layer: only one or two light but resilient wind-catching panels.
- Two to four rear hems follow the body a fraction late.
- The back reads first as one clean color mass. Fine decoration is limited to one zone—shoulder/back-waist, rear collar, cuff, or hem—in low-contrast pattern, thin gold line, transparent embossing, or woven line.
- Palette: moon white, frost gray, mist blue, pale indigo, sky blue, pale green, light peach, soft apricot, pale lotus purple, pearl white, pale champagne, light ochre glow, restrained soft vermilion.
- Women are long, light, and quietly strong; men are clean, tall, upright, or moderately robust. Both share the same design philosophy.

## Light and physical realism

High-altitude sky acts as a huge softbox. Highlights are warm and detailed; shadows are bright and transparent. Distant architecture fades through air. Stone has mass; jade is warm; timber and tile construction are legible; pine bark has age; shallow water has surface tension; clouds have volume; fabric obeys gravity, translucency, drape, and wind; moon texture is astronomical rather than painted.

## Global exclusions

No text, captions, logos, watermarks, grids, borders, modern objects, identifiable historical dynasty, throne-room clutter, dense floating city, fantasy game HUD, neon cyberpunk color, excessive gold ornament, blank white band, flat pasted moon, cloud covering the person's feet, railing, warped architecture, duplicated person, extra limb, fused garment, aggressive lens flare, dutch angle, or poster layout.

The desired read order is: impossible palace scale → complete moon/bridge/river/cloud world → tiny rear-view immortal → clothing, pine, corridor, waterfall, and light leading deeper into the unseen realm.
